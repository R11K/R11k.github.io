SMART Goals

Overview

These are the SMART personal-development goals I set for my co-op work term at STEM Minds Corp, organized under three of the undergraduate competencies at the core of my program. My placement runs from May through August 2026, so the goals below are actively in progress; each notes where I currently stand.

Competency 01

Critical Thinking & Problem Solving

Goal 1.1 — Develop practical engineering skills in robotics and embedded systems

Design, build, and test systems across the robotics and embedded platforms used at STEM Minds: automated grow kits on Micro:bit V2 and Raspberry Pi, small farm robots, Climate Tree sensor nodes, and support for drone operations — producing a short design rationale for each.

In progress

Goal 1.2 — Develop systematic troubleshooting and testing skills

Build a structured troubleshooting process across sensors, communication links, motor controllers, power systems, and software — diagnosing and documenting technical issues and tracking recurring failures so they don’t repeat.

In progress
Competency 02

Collaboration Across Networks & Leading With Influence

Goal 2.1 — Build industry-ready project management and documentation skills

Manage my own schedule to hit deadlines, track work on Linear, and maintain build logs, test records, and bills of materials so any teammate can pick up my work — producing formal technical documents reviewed by my supervisor.

In progress

Goal 2.2 — Improve team coordination and leadership through project ownership

Take ownership of at least one significant project or major component from planning through completion, coordinating across software, hardware, and education teammates and leading through influence rather than authority.

In progress
Competency 03

Effective Oral & Written Communication

Goal 3.1 — Build communication and mentorship skills through student and educator engagement

Translate technical documentation into clear instructions and visual explanations for non-technical audiences, and help deliver workshops and classroom sessions on robotics and programming for students and teachers.

In progress

Goal 3.2 — Improve professional written and oral technical communication

Prepare structured status updates, write concise project reports, present progress to the team, and actively seek feedback on my communication so it keeps improving across the term.

In progress

Full SMART goal breakdowns (Specific, Measurable, Attainable, Relevant, Time-Bound) are maintained in my COOP 2100 learning portfolio.
